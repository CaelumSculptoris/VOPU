# E01 — ImpCarv Cavity-Channel Guidance

## Objective

Test whether a central hollow mode in a straight, elongated ImpCarv-written cavity remains confined after the complete exposure, wash, solvent-exchange, shrinkage, and drying process. Record the intermediate liquid-filled cavity and verify liquid removal and final gas filling. Use open-ended channels accessible to wash/exchange fluid, and document endpoint connectivity. Compare a plain lumen with structured cavity/web boundaries intended to reduce leakage. The final gas core is lower-index than the surrounding gel: the discontinuity reflects and reshapes light, but it is not ordinary step-index TIR back into the cavity.

## Inputs

- Sample ID and design revision.
- Guide length, cross-section, wavelength, and coupling geometry.
- Lumen and surrounding-vacancy cross-section, measured refractive indices, endpoint connectivity, and complete exposure, wash/developer, solvent-exchange, ionic-shrinkage, and supercritical-drying history.
- Reference path and calibration standard.

## Measurements

Record cross-section and post-shrink dimensions; endpoint connectivity; evidence of residual-liquid removal and final gas-filled lumen; input/output power; near-field mode profile confirming power in the hollow lumen; phase where available; wavelength sweep; environmental conditions; and replicate number. Measure transmission for multiple guide lengths and fit loss versus length (cutback) to separate propagation loss from facet coupling. Include a plain-lumen control and a structured-boundary candidate.

## Acceptance criteria

Before measurement, define maximum propagation loss, crosstalk, and mode impurity for the intended route length and application. The existing < 0.05 dB/cm value may be retained as a project target only if justified and registered in advance; it is not an expected or literature-established VOPU result. Report pass, fail, or inconclusive, and do not change the threshold after seeing data without a decision-log entry.
